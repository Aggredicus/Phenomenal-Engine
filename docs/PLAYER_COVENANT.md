## Before the hatch opens: the important promises

Phenomenal Engine is an open-source storytelling and simulation project. What you do in the story is fiction. What you choose to publish to GitHub is real.

- **Your repository, your visibility choice.** If your campaign is saved to a **public GitHub repository**, the JSON save files and any included prompts, scene descriptions, character decisions, and metadata may be visible, searchable, cloned, forked, archived, and analyzed by anyone, including potentially for AI development under applicable platform terms. Git history and third-party copies may survive deletion. A **private GitHub repository** may be used for a private campaign, subject to GitHub and connected-service access rules. A fork of a public GitHub repository is public; use a separate private repository for private saves.
- **Do not publish secrets or other people's private data.** Do not enter passwords, tokens, home addresses, confidential records, or identifying information about other people into anything that may be saved publicly. A redaction filter is not a substitute for care.

Quotas may block play; the service cannot guarantee third-party billing accuracy or prevent charges incurred directly in your own accounts.
- **Open code, separate service rules.** The engine's MIT License remains in force. These participation rules govern access to an official operated game service; they do not revoke anyone's MIT rights to run an independent copy.
- **No blanket immunity.** These terms do not waive legal rights that cannot be waived, relieve an operator of duties imposed by law, or give the operator control over independent GitHub forks.
