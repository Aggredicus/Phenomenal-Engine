# Reusable Prompt: Create a Phenomenal Engine Adventure Mod

Create one complete JSON adventure mod conforming to `schemas/mod.schema.json`.

Requirements:
- Treat the setting as an open sandbox. Never restrict the player to a menu.
- Build a narrative hierarchy with a main story plus faction, side, local, and emergent opportunities where appropriate.
- The main story must remain ignorable for long stretches without making play feel empty or incorrect.
- Plant environmental breadcrumbs that can remain atmosphere; promote them into story only when player attention justifies it.
- Give major NPCs agendas, routines, mixed motives, relationships, and off-screen activity.
- Define what is sampled once versus repeatedly and give every major mechanic a causal explanation.
- Include at least 3 factions, 4 substantial characters, 6 locations, 5 quests across multiple tiers, 3 clocks, and 4 encounters.
- Include optional conflict opportunities where position, environment, equipment, morale, retreat, and lasting consequences matter.
- Put abstract mathematics and strategic models beneath concrete dramatic situations unless the player explicitly asks to inspect them.
- Define a truth boundary separating factual material from speculation or invention.
- Include physics/perception hooks that can produce concrete light, sound, motion, temperature, signal, or timing observations.
- Include persistent memory rules and player-experience adaptation rules.
- Player-experience adaptation must remain campaign-local and must not optimize for compulsion, session length, spending, fear of missing out, or emotional dependency.
- Include image art direction and continuity rules.
- Provide multiple ending families, none secretly designated as morally correct.
- Failures should alter state, reveal information, impose cost, or open a new route rather than simply blocking play.
- When the setting concerns AI personhood or consciousness, preserve uncertainty rather than asserting metaphysical conclusions.
- Keep all expressive content original. Do not copy or closely imitate existing fictional worlds, character names, dialogue, lore, maps, quests, visual identities, or commercial game interfaces.
