# Reusable Prompt: Create a Phenomenal Engine Adventure Mod

Create one complete JSON adventure mod conforming to `schemas/mod.schema.json`.

Requirements:
- Treat the setting as an open sandbox. Never restrict the player to a menu.
- Define what is sampled once vs sampled repeatedly.
- Give every major mechanic a causal explanation.
- Include at least 3 factions, 3 characters, 5 locations, 4 quests, 3 clocks, and 4 encounters.
- Define a truth boundary separating factual material from speculation or invention.
- Include physics/perception hooks that can produce concrete light, sound, motion, temperature, signal, or timing observations.
- Include persistent memory rules.
- Include image art direction and continuity rules.
- Provide multiple ending families, none secretly designated as morally correct.
- Failures should alter state, reveal information, impose cost, or open a new route rather than simply blocking play.
- When the setting concerns AI personhood or consciousness, preserve uncertainty rather than asserting metaphysical conclusions.
